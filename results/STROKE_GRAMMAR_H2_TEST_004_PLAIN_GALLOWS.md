Stroke Grammar H2 test 004 — plain gallows replication

Dataset: 18,211 ordinary t/k/p/f events; all c[t/k/p/f]h pedestal occurrences excluded.
Five whole-folio holdout folds.

An additive aaa q/l x p/x component model, forbidden to memorize t/k/p/f combinations, retained the following fractions of the unfactored four-category model's held-out contextual gain:
- previous character 93.67%
- following character 97.37%
- within-word position 93.83%
- word length 91.65%

Both whole and additive models improved over baseline in all five folds for all four targets.

Interpretation: the same external aaa component factorization previously observed in pedestalled gallows replicates in ordinary gallows after pedestal evidence is removed. This is the third component-family replication after pedestal and minim-tail.

No semantic or phonetic values are inferred.
